Three rules the pages enforce, not the ops team

Collection is confirmed, never assumed. The hub and share date are a checkbox at checkout, not small print, because a member who cannot reach the hub becomes a share day no show and a refund argument.
A late payment is never bounced. Every payment screen states what happens on late, short, double and wrong reference before the member pays, and each of those paths ends in slots or credit.
Escrow is not operating cash. The reconciliation page shows available cash with escrow excluded, so nobody in the company ever sees a number that tempts them.
