JOIN A POOL

How it works, including the parts that go wrong

Four steps, then the three situations people actually worry about.

01
Find a pool at a hub you can reach

Each pool names one hub and one share date. Check you can be there before you pay, because this is collection, not delivery.

02
Pay for your slot in full

One bank transfer to an account that belongs to you. No instalments, no card stored, no credit. You have 20 minutes from reserving.

03
Wait for the pool to fill

We buy nothing until enough people have joined. You can name other people to collect your slots, and change those names until the pool locks.

04
Collect in your booked window

Read your code, watch your portion weighed, take it home. Twenty minute windows so nobody queues behind forty people.

The three things people ask

What if it does not fill?

Nothing is bought and everyone is refunded in full, to the account they paid from, within 24 hours. Every pool shows the number it needs and how far off it is.

What if my portion is small?

Meat pools publish a tolerance band, usually ±8%. Come in under it and we credit you the difference automatically, before you leave the hub. You do not have to complain first.

Can I send someone else?

Yes, and most people do. Name them and they get their own code and their own message. Refunds still go back to whoever paid.

The pool policy, in plain words

THRESHOLDEvery pool names the minimum number of members it needs. Below that at closing time, it is cancelled.
PRICEThe price you see is the price you pay. If the farmer raises his price after you have joined, that is our loss, not yours.
PULLING OUTYou can withdraw for a full refund until 72 hours before the pool closes. After that your money is committed to the purchase.
WHERE IT SITSMembers' money is in a separate bank account. It moves twice only: to the supplier when the pool funds, or back to you.
SHORT WEIGHTUnder the published tolerance band, the difference is credited automatically. You can ask for it as cash instead.
NOT AN INVESTMENTYou are buying food at a lower price. There is no return, no interest and no scheme. Anyone who tells you otherwise is not us.
