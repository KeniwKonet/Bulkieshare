DATA ROOM

Every naira we hold belongs to someone who has not received their food yet.

BulkieShare pools money from members of Abuja cooperatives to buy food at wholesale, then splits and hands it over at a neighbourhood hub. The hard part is not the marketplace. It is running a segregated escrow, a double entry ledger and a published refund SLA well enough that a failed pool does not end the business.

This page is the argument for why that discipline is the product.

The component the whole model rests on

A progress bar that lies is a business risk. Ours shows paid slots as solid fill and unpaid reservations as hatching, so it never moves backwards when a hold expires.

Beside it sits the threshold, the deadline and the refund promise. Three facts that answer the only question a first time buyer has, which is what happens to my money if this fails.

Where the money sits, at every hour of a pool

Members' funds enter a segregated holding account and are only debited on two events: paying the supplier once the pool is funded, or refunding the member. The dashboard our team looks at every morning shows operating cash with escrow excluded, so the temptation never presents itself.

01 · OPEN
pool_escrow

Payment lands, ledger credits escrow, member sees a confirmed slot. Nothing has been spent.

02 · FUNDED
supplier_payable

Threshold met. A 40% deposit is released against a purchase order and a quote that has not expired.

03 · UNDERFILLED
refunding

Threshold missed. Reversing entries return every naira to the payer. No approval meeting, no queue.

04 · COMPLETED
revenue:margin

Revenue is recognised only after the last handover, and never exceeds collected minus supplier cost minus logistics.

The competitor slide, corrected

Pricepally has run group buying of bulk food in Nigeria since 2019, has raised roughly $1.7M, and already operates in Abuja alongside Lagos, Ibadan and Port Harcourt. Any deck that names only NKATAA is not describing the market.

Our two defensible edges are hub collection, which removes the delivery cost line that kills grocery margins here, and the cooperative coordinator as the acquisition unit rather than the individual shopper. One coordinator brings twelve members at a customer acquisition cost close to zero.
